Decoding the Digital Noise

We are ByteHunters. We build the tools that separate signal from static and truth from fiction.

The Mission

In an era where information travels faster than verification, the line between fact and fabrication has blurred. We refused to accept that.


Born in the dorms of IIT Bombay, ByteHunters started as a late-night debate about AI ethics and evolved into a robust system designed to restore trust in digital media. By combining advanced Web Scraping with the reasoning capabilities of Large Language Models (Gemini), we provide a transparent layer of verification for everyone.
